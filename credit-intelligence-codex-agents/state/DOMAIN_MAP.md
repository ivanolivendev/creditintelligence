# DOMAIN MAP

## Core concepts

Proposal → immutable DocumentVersion → DocumentSetSnapshot → Analysis → Extraction/Finding/Assessment → optional AIExplanation → human Decision.

## Financial invariants verified

- Missing, ambiguous and invalid facts are distinct from zero.
- Complete financial assessment requires three distinct matching payslip and statement periods.
- Incomplete factual income cannot produce positive income consistency or stability findings.
- Semantic duplicate salary credits require review and are not summed into a match.
- Java remains the authority for deterministic calculations and decision state.
- A decision references a completed analysis and the reviewed document snapshot.

## Policy

Policies are versioned and checksum-validated. The demo policy is synthetic and does not represent a real lending policy.
