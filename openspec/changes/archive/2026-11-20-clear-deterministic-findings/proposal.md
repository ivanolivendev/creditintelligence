# Clear deterministic finding feedback

## Why

The analyst workspace currently exposes internal finding codes and raw rule strings such as `Declared=8000.00...` and `Required documents present`. These are difficult to act on and obscure the evidence and policy that produced each result.

## Scope

Present deterministic income and required-document findings in plain Portuguese with their status, relevant values or document types, evidence origin, and a clear next step. Keep rule evaluation authoritative in Java and the final decision with the analyst.

## Out of scope

Changing thresholds, policy outcomes, extraction behavior, AI-generated findings, or credit decisions.
